The basics

What is SartorialAI?

SartorialAI is an AI revenue system for growing businesses. It combines an AI front desk, lead generation and outreach, a content engine, and operations automation in one platform, all trained on your business and connected to the tools you already use.

What can the AI agents actually do?

They take action, not just answer questions. Agents answer calls and messages, book appointments, qualify and contact leads, draft and publish content, request reviews, update your CRM and run scheduled tasks overnight. Each one is trained on your services, policies and voice.

Which AI models are included?

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more. New models are added shortly after they launch and are included in your plan.

What is the AI Co-Founder?

A daily assistant that prepares your sales and marketing work for you: content, prospect outreach, follow-ups and reply drafts. Each morning you review a short queue and approve, edit or skip each item. Nothing goes out without your OK unless you turn on autopilot.

Can SartorialAI help me show up in ChatGPT answers?

Yes. AI search visibility monitoring checks what ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and DeepSeek say when people ask about businesses like yours, alerts you when a competitor gains ground, and tells you what to publish to close the gap.

Can I see a demo first?

Yes. Book a demo and we will walk through SartorialAI on your own business, or start free and see it work on your website today. Larger teams and multi-location businesses can talk to sales about custom setups.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Guided onboarding builds your customer profile, lead list, outreach, content plan and website agent for you. Day to day, you direct everything in plain English.

Platform and integrations

Does it work with the tools I already use?

Yes. SartorialAI connects to 1,000+ apps including Salesforce, HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Slack, LinkedIn, Notion and Shopify, plus native n8n workflows, a REST API and MCP access.

Can it answer my phones and support customers?

Yes. AI receptionists and support agents work across phone, SMS, WhatsApp, web chat and email around the clock. They qualify leads, book appointments, answer questions and update your CRM, and they hand complex issues to your team with full context.

Can I run it without opening the app?
