FAQ

What law firms ask us most.

Still have a question? Email morris@sartorialai.co.

Can SartorialAI handle legal intake and qualify cases?

Yes. Zane asks your configured intake questions consistently, captures the nature of the matter, flags potential conflicts and high-value cases, and screens out matters outside your practice areas.

Does it book consultations into my calendar?

Yes. It offers your real consultation availability, books directly into your calendar and sends reminders to reduce no-shows.

Will it sound professional enough for my firm?

You set the tone, practice areas and intake language, so every call represents your firm consistently, including after hours and overflow.

Does it give legal advice?

No. It gathers facts, answers logistics questions you approve, and books time with an attorney. You decide exactly what it can and can't say.

How quickly can a firm get started?

Most firms are fully live within 7 days, and we do the setup: we connect your calendar, load your practice areas and intake questions, and run test calls with you before launch.
